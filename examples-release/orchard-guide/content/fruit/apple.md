# Growing Apples

Apples prefer an open position with plenty of winter sun. Mulch after the first
autumn rain and prune lightly once the tree is dormant.

## Seasonal checklist

- Winter: prune crossing branches
- Spring: watch for codling moth
- Summer: water deeply during dry weeks
- Autumn: harvest when fruit twists free

## Harvest notes

| Variety      | Harvest | Keeps for |
| :----------- | :-----: | --------: |
| Gravenstein  | January |   2 weeks |
| Pink Lady    | April   |  12 weeks |
| Granny Smith | May     |  16 weeks |

## Keeping templates beside an article

Ordinary fenced blocks are escaped safely:

```sh

```

The renderer can include a complete neighboring source file:

```roc
file:template-example.roc
```

Or only a named snippet:

```roc
file:template-example.roc:snippet:view
```
