# Growing Cherries

Cherries reward good netting and patient pruning. Most varieties prefer a cold
winter and a dry spell while the fruit ripens.

## Protecting the crop

- Net before the fruit colours.
- Keep the net taut and clear of the ground.
- Pick split fruit promptly after rain.
- Share a small unnetted branch with the local birds.

Sweet cherries are best eaten on the day they are picked.
