# Growing Bananas

Bananas are giant herbs rather than trees. In a cool climate they need a
sheltered wall or glasshouse, rich compost, and reliable summer water.

## Planting

1. Choose the warmest position available.
2. Enrich the soil with compost.
3. Plant after the last cold night.
4. Keep one strong follower beside each fruiting stem.

Fruit is ready when the ridges soften and the flower end brushes away easily.
